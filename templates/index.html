How to read the answers

grounded unsupported contradicted

Every sentence is checked against the passages the BiLSTM retrieved. Hallucination = unsupported + contradicted claims; lower is better. Personalisation = how much of this patient's own record the answer actually uses.

LSTM Only returns a passage verbatim, so it should read close to 0% hallucination — that is the built-in check that the detector works. It scores near zero on personalisation for the same reason, and finishes last on overall quality in the thesis evaluation.

Live scores use the retrieved passages only; the evaluation in outputs/ also folds in the gold MedQuAD answer, so the two sets of numbers are not directly comparable.
